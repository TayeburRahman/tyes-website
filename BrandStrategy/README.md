## Pricing model (single source of truth)

**5 pricing tiers:**
- **Free Image** — $0 (with **+$25 add-on** button for Brand Strategy)
- **Brand Strategy** — $25 standalone (own card in pricing)
- **Campaign 5** *(Popular)* — $25 (5 images + FREE strategy bundled)
- **Campaign 10** — $45 (10 images + FREE strategy bundled)
- **Custom Enterprise** — Get in Touch (custom scope + FREE strategy + 30-min discovery call)

**Brand Strategy Snapshot** — 3 ways to get it:
1. FREE bundled with Campaign 5, Campaign 10, or Custom
2. $25 add-on on Free Image
3. $25 standalone Brand Strategy tier

**Deep Dive Brand Strategy** — bespoke engagement, priced per scope, subject to availability. Separate from the $25 Snapshot. Contact / Book a Call for quote.

## Design system

**Fonts:** League Spartan (titles) · Montserrat Medium (body + buttons) · Montserrat Italic (accents) · Gliker (brand mark)

**Colors:** `#2DD4BF` teal accent (CTAs · "+ Add Brand Strategy $25" add-on toggle · terminal states) · `#FBBF24` yellow (dev warnings + pending states + rate-limit notices ONLY — never on the customer-facing add-on toggle) · `#0A0A0A` / `#141414` / `#1a1a1a` dark surfaces

**Global rules:** No em-dashes · No NEW badges · No email in strategy delivery copy · "Our graphic designers" (not human retouchers) · Snapshot delivered to dashboard only

**Calendly events:** 2 separate — `discovery-call` (30 min, Custom Enterprise) · `deep-dive-call` (1 hour, Deep Dive customers)
