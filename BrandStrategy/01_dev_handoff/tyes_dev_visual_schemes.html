4 · Deep Dive Buyer Introductions · Off-Platform (NO in-app pipeline)

Deep Dive customers get warm buyer introductions off-platform via email after the kickoff call. No in-app Buyer Intros workflow — Sheika manages buyer relationships and follow-through personally by email.

What NOT to build
Do not build any of the following:
✕ Buyer Intros sidebar item
✕ Buyer Intros list view / status pipeline (Draft · Sent · Buyer Replied · Meeting Booked · Deal Closed · No Response)
✕ Buyer contact database / retailer notes UI
✕ Intro email templates in-app
✕ Commission tracking field or admin UI
✕ buyer_intros DB table
✕ /api/admin/buyer-intros endpoints
✕ Analytics KPIs "Buyer Intros Sent" and "Intro → Deal conversion"
What actually happens:
1. Client books Deep Dive kickoff call (Calendly deep-dive-call, 1-hour)
2. After the call, Sheika prepares the buyer list and intro emails offline
3. All buyer intros, follow-ups, and outcome reporting happen by email between Sheika and the client
4. No status is written back to the platform; the platform is not the source of truth for the buyer relationship
