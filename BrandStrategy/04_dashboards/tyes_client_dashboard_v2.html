Section 0 · Is Free-then-Charge a Good Idea?

Short answer: Yes, when the free version delivers real value but stops short of execution. That's exactly what tyes does. And it's how every successful freemium company in this category operates.

Why brands don't feel tricked

Trust before the sale. A brand that has never heard of tyes won't pay for strategy on day one. They'll pay after they've seen one piece of work that proves the sharpness.

Diagnosis vs. cure. The free Snapshot identifies gaps (LLM invisibility, missing viral SKUs, weak distribution). Identifying gaps is valuable. Solving them is the Deep Dive Brand Strategy.

Loss aversion. Once brands see the gap, doing nothing feels like leaving money on the table. Upgrading to Deep Dive becomes the natural next step.

Same pattern that already works. Ahrefs (free SEO audits, paid SEO tools). HubSpot (free website grader, paid CRM). Klaviyo (free email reports, paid platform). Same playbook for tyes brand strategy.

The 5 sections in detail

"You have X Snapshots delivered · Y in progress. Last updated: [date]." If no snapshot yet, empty state: "You don't have a Brand Strategy Snapshot yet. Start with a Campaign 5 to get one included free, add the $25 Snapshot add-on to your Free Image, or order the standalone Brand Strategy tier for $25."

Same Brand Info form as Step 3 of New Order. Standalone use case: user wants an updated snapshot without a new image order (e.g. brand has evolved). Default state: collapsed. Click to expand. Requesting standalone triggers the $25 Brand Strategy checkout before the request is submitted (unless user has an active Campaign order in which the snapshot is bundled free).

Per row: brand name · date · page count · status badge · download PDF button. Empty state: "Your delivered snapshots will appear here."
