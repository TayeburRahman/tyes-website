Frequently asked.

Are packages one-time or subscription?

All packages are one-time. Pay per campaign. Scale when ready.

What's the Brand Strategy Snapshot?

A custom snapshot tailored to your brand: LLM visibility gaps (where you show up in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity), viral product angles for your niche, and a shortlist of retail buyers who fit your brand. Delivered to your dashboard within 3 business days.

How do I get the Brand Strategy Snapshot?

Three ways: included FREE with any Campaign 5, Campaign 10, or Custom order. Add it to Free Image for $25. Or order the Brand Strategy tier standalone for $25. Same snapshot in all three paths.

What's the difference between the Deep Dive Brand Strategy and the Brand Strategy Snapshot?

Two very different products. The Brand Strategy Snapshot ($25) is a 3-day audit that identifies your gaps: LLM visibility, viral product angles, retail buyer shortlist. Same snapshot whether you get it free with a Campaign, as a $25 add-on, or as the $25 standalone tier.

The Deep Dive Brand Strategy is a bespoke engagement that solves those gaps: full LLM audit with a 90-day implementation roadmap, viral product concepts developed for your niche, warm intros to retail buyers we already work with personally, packaging and positioning direction, and a 1-hour strategy call. Priced per scope, subject to availability. Contact Us or Book a Call to get started.

Short version: Snapshot = diagnosis. Deep Dive = cure + access.

Is there a free trial available?

Yes. Free Image is $0. 1 Free Image per user. After that, order a Campaign or grab the standalone Brand Strategy tier.

How long does delivery take?

Free Image: from 3 hours. Campaign 5 & Campaign 10: from 24 hours. Custom: handled per scope. Strategy snapshot: 3 business days.

Can I request revisions on my images?

Campaign 5 and Campaign 10 include 1 revision per image. Custom revisions are agreed per scope.

What categories do you support?

Beauty. Personal Care. Fragrance. Fashion.

What if I need images faster?

Add your deadline directly in your brief when you place the order. We'll do our best to accommodate and confirm turnaround before starting.
