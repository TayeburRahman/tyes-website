/* React component */
import { brands } from '@/data/brands';

export default function BrandsMarquee() {

    <section className="brands-marquee">
      <p className="eyebrow">Few of the brands we've worked with</p>

How it works: The trick is duplicating the logo list ([...brands, ...brands]) and animating translateX(0) → translateX(-50%). Updated FAQ #1, "What is tyes and how does it work?" (borrows About Us language)
tyes is a two-engine platform. AI campaign imagery and brand strategy backed by a retail buyer network across the US and Europe, built for founders and marketing teams who need to go from idea to shelf in weeks, not quarters. AI does the heavy lifting. Real experts do the fine work. Strategy is baked in from the first brief. And when you're ready, we put your product in front of the retail buyers we already work with, the ones actually deciding what lands on the shelf next season.
Updated FAQ #2, "What's the Brand Strategy Snapshot?"
A custom snapshot tailored to your brand. LLM visibility audit (where you show up in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity). Viral product angles for your niche. Retailer shortlist across our 5 buyer categories (mass market, premium, hyperstores, hyperpharmacies, marketplaces) across the US and Europe. Delivered to your dashboard within 3 business days. Updated FAQ #3, "How do I get the Brand Strategy Snapshot?"
Three ways: included FREE with every Campaign 5, Campaign 10, or Custom order. Add it to Free Image for $25. Or order the Brand Strategy tier standalone for $25. Same snapshot in all three paths.
NEW FAQ #4, "What's the difference between the Deep Dive Brand Strategy and the Brand Strategy Snapshot?"
Two very different products.

Brand Strategy Snapshot ($25) is a 3-day audit that identifies your gaps: where you show up (or don't) in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity; the viral product angles for your niche; and a shortlist of retail buyers who fit your brand. Same snapshot whether you get it free with a Campaign, as a $25 add-on on Free Image, or as the $25 standalone tier.

Deep Dive Brand Strategy is a bespoke engagement that solves the gaps. Full LLM audit with a 90-day implementation roadmap. Viral product concepts developed for your niche. Warm intros to retail buyers we already work with, personally. Packaging and positioning direction. 1-hour strategy call. Priced per scope, subject to availability. Contact Us or Book a Call to get started.

Short version: Snapshot = diagnosis. Deep Dive = cure + access.
