Admin action button definitions
Change status — manually move the request through New → In Progress → Ready to Send → Sent → Lost.
Upload PDF — attach Raluca's completed Snapshot PDF (3-5 pages) to the request record. Once uploaded, it's visible in the client dashboard.
Send to client — publish the Snapshot to the client's dashboard AND fire an in-app + email notification ("Your Brand Strategy Snapshot is ready"). Auto-updates status to "Sent".
Flag for Deep Dive nudge — for brands Raluca thinks are strong Deep Dive candidates (retail-ready, opportunity is bigger than a Snapshot can address). Clicking it: (1) marks the request as "Converted to Deep Dive" in the pipeline for tracking; (2) fires an automated nudge email to the client with a soft "Ready for the full playbook? Book a Deep Dive call →" CTA (opens Calendly deep-dive-call event). It does NOT create a Deep Dive engagement — the client still has to book the call. Think of it as: "this brand is ripe, send the nudge."
Section 4 · Deep Dive Buyer Introductions · Off-Platform

No in-app module. Buyer introductions and follow-through for Deep Dive customers are handled off-platform via email after the Deep Dive kickoff call. Sheika manages the buyer relationships personally and communicates outcomes to the client directly by email. Nothing about buyer intros is uploaded, tracked, or exposed in the admin dashboard.

Rationale: Buyer relationships are high-touch and sensitive. Managing them off-platform keeps the process flexible, protects buyer contact details, and avoids the maintenance overhead of an internal CRM. The platform's promise ("warm intros to retail buyers we know personally") is delivered through Sheika's network, not through in-app messaging.
What NOT to build
No Buyer Intros sidebar item. No Buyer Intros list view. No intro-email templates in-app. No buyer contact database. No status pipeline (Sent · Replied · Meeting Booked, etc.). No commission tracking UI. No API endpoints under /api/admin/buyer-intros.
